Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, supportive, and Christ-centered home where women can heal, grow, and rebuild their lives in recovery. Through faith, accountability, compassion, and community, we empower each resident to overcome addiction, restore her sense of purpose, and develop the spiritual, emotional, and practical foundations needed for lasting sobriety.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see every woman who enters our home experience lasting freedom from addiction through the transforming power of faith, discovering her God-given identity, purpose, and hope. We envision a community where women are restored in spirit, mind, and body, equipped to live healthy, sober, and independent lives.

From the Director

A heartfelt personal message from Founder and Director Holly Brannon.

🕊️ A Heart for Restoration & Ministry
My desire to help women in addiction began in 2018 when my older sister died of a drug overdose. She had struggled with addiction for many years. I wanted to help her, but didn’t know how. I prayed and talked to her about how I had overcome the same kind of things through my faith in Jesus Christ to deliver us. She started her journey of deeper faith that year. I opened my first Hope and Haven House, August 1, 2025 in Elsmere, Kentucky. It is a safe place for women to recover from addiction and move toward a beautiful life of sobriety.
Holly Brannon
Founder & Director, Hope and Haven Houses LLC
Founded August 1, 2025 • Elsmere, Kentucky
