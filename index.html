Program Expectations

Our foundation is built on personal responsibility, faith, and community structure.

Nightly Curfew

Structure brings stability. All residents must adhere to strict nightly curfews to support a routine, restful environment.

Bible Class & Worship

Spiritual growth is central to long-term freedom. Weekly attendance at house Bible classes and services is mandatory.

Employment Search

Financial independence and purpose are critical. All non-disabled residents must actively seek and secure gainful employment.

Resident Application Form

Please complete all fields and acknowledge the house agreements below.

1. Curfew Policy Agreement

I understand that Hope Haven through Jesus enforces a strict curfew (10:00 PM Sunday–Thursday, 11:00 PM Friday–Saturday). Failure to meet curfew without prior leadership approval may result in disciplinary action or removal from the house.

2. Mandatory Bible Class Agreement

I acknowledge that Hope Haven through Jesus is a Christ-centered ministry. I agree to regularly attend and participate in all mandatory weekly Bible classes, devotions, and designated community meetings.

3. Job Finding & Work Agreement

I agree to actively seek gainful employment or engage in approved vocational training/community service within 14 days of entry. I commit to providing weekly proof of job applications until employed.
