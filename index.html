Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, supportive, and Christ-centered home where women can heal, grow, and rebuild their lives in recovery. Through faith, accountability, compassion, and community, we empower each resident to overcome addiction, restore her sense of purpose, and develop the spiritual, emotional, and practical foundations needed for lasting sobriety.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see every woman who enters our home experience lasting freedom from addiction through the transforming power of faith, discovering her God-given identity, purpose, and hope. We envision a community where women are restored in spirit, mind, and body, equipped to live healthy, sober, and independent lives.

🌸 Are You or Your Loved One Ready for Hope & Haven?

Our home is built for women who are eager to commit to active recovery and faith-based community living:

✓ Completed medical detox or inpatient treatment (or currently maintaining sobriety).
✓ Willing to submit to random, observed drug and alcohol screenings.
✓ Committed to attending house devotions, 12-step/recovery meetings, and Sunday services.
✓ Ready to obtain full-time employment or pursue approved education/outpatient therapy.

Standards & Daily Routine

Peace flourishes in an orderly, Christ-centered home with clear expectations.

🌸 A Typical Day at Hope & Haven

Consistency and routine build the self-discipline necessary for long-term sobriety.

7:00 AM – 8:30 AM

Morning Devotions, Prayer & Chores

Start the day with God's word, personal reflection, breakfast, and designated house chore rotations.

9:00 AM – 4:30 PM

Work, IOP, Job Search & Community Service

Residents report to full-time employment, clinical outpatient therapies (IOP), or active scheduled job searches.

5:30 PM – 7:00 PM

Family Dinner & Fellowship

Gather in the kitchen to prepare meals together, unwind, and build positive sisterhood connections.

7:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Recovery Meetings & Weekly House Bible Class

10:00 PM (11:00 PM Weekends)

Curfew & Quiet Hours

All residents signed in for curfew. Doors locked for maximum resident safety and restful sleep.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about residency and admission requirements.

What are the program fees and rent costs? +
Program fees include fully furnished living spaces, high-speed WiFi, laundry, kitchen amenities, and utilities. Please contact Holly Brannon directly or submit an application for current weekly bed fees and available scholarship slots.
Are personal cell phones and vehicles permitted? +
Yes. Cell phones are permitted outside of mandatory meeting times and quiet hours. Residents with valid driver’s licenses, proof of active insurance, and registration may bring their vehicles.
Can my family or friends visit me at the house? +
No. We enforce a strict NO VISITORS ON THE PROPERTY rule at all times to ensure the privacy, physical safety, and recovery focus of all residents. Passes to visit family off-property are earned after the initial 30-day blackout period.
What should I bring on move-in day? +
Bring approximately 1–2 weeks of casual and interview clothing, personal toiletries (unopened, non-alcoholic), any prescribed medications (in original pharmacy bottles), and a Bible/notebook. Bedding, pillows, and kitchen supplies are fully provided.
