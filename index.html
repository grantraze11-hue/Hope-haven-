Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, supportive, and Christ-centered home where women can heal, grow, and rebuild their lives in recovery. Through faith, accountability, compassion, and community, we empower each resident to overcome addiction, restore her sense of purpose, and develop the spiritual, emotional, and practical foundations needed for lasting sobriety.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see every woman who enters our home experience lasting freedom from addiction through the transforming power of faith, discovering her God-given identity, purpose, and hope. We envision a community where women are restored in spirit, mind, and body, equipped to live healthy, sober, and independent lives.

🌸 Are You or Your Loved One Ready for Hope & Haven?

Our home is built for women who are eager to commit to active recovery and faith-based community living:

✓ Completed medical detox or inpatient treatment (or currently maintaining sobriety).
✓ Willing to submit to random, observed drug and alcohol screenings.
✓ Committed to attending house devotions, 12-step/recovery meetings, and Sunday services.
✓ Ready to obtain full-time employment or pursue approved education/outpatient therapy.
