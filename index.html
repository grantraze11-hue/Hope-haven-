Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, supportive, and Christ-centered home where women can heal, grow, and rebuild their lives in recovery. Through faith, accountability, compassion, and community, we empower each resident to overcome addiction, restore her sense of purpose, and develop the spiritual, emotional, and practical foundations needed for lasting sobriety. We are committed to walking alongside every woman with grace, hope, and love as she embraces a transformed life rooted in faith, dignity, and service to others.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see every woman who enters our home experience lasting freedom from addiction through the transforming power of faith, discovering her God-given identity, purpose, and hope. We envision a community where women are restored in spirit, mind, and body, equipped to live healthy, sober, and independent lives, and empowered to strengthen their families, serve their communities, and inspire others through lives marked by faith, resilience, and love.

Program Standards & Culture

Built upon Biblical foundation, personal accountability, and community structure.

🌸 Nightly Curfew

Structure establishes peace. All residents adhere to nightly curfews to foster a stable, safe, and restful home environment.

🌸 Bible Class & Worship

Faith is our core foundation. Attendance at weekly house Bible classes and devotions is mandatory for spiritual growth.

🌸 Employment Search

Independence creates self-worth. Non-disabled residents must actively seek and maintain full-time gainful employment.

🌸 Clean Living Space

Respect for our shared home is essential. Weekly chore rotations and tidy personal living areas are strictly maintained.

🌸 Random Drug Screenings

Zero-tolerance safety. Regular screenings safeguard the recovery journey for all women living in the house.

🌸 Strict Property Rules

No unauthorized visitors allowed on property at any time to guarantee safety, privacy, and full focus on recovery.
